The Casper app

What Casper does on your computer

It lives in your menu bar (Mac) or tray (Windows). A small ghost. It runs quietly in the background; there's nothing to do day to day.

It keeps the folders you choose mirrored on your friends' computers: every change, within seconds, with 30 days of history so anything can be brought back.

Your files are encrypted on your computer before they leave it. Friends hold copies they can never read, and so does Casper.

It only works inside your home folder, and only on folders you've chosen. It never touches anything else.

You confirm anything important in Casper's own dialogs on your computer, never by chat.

To remove it, on a Mac choose Quit in its menu and drag Casper from Applications to the Trash; on Windows open Settings, Apps, find Casper and choose Uninstall. Friends' copies of your folders are deleted after 7 days.

On a Mac it is signed by its developer and notarized by Apple. Mirroring runs on Syncthing, an open-source sync engine.
