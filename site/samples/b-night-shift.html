// how it works

One conversation. Then it's plumbing.

01

Point your agent

It installs Casper, asks a couple of questions, and connects itself.

02

Invite your people

Friends give space. Always-on machines can catch changes while laptops sleep.

03

You confirm once

In a dialog your agent can't click. Every change after that is mirrored in seconds.

// trust model

Hold each other's data. Never read it.

e2e

Sealed on your Mac

Names, structure, contents. Peers store ciphertext. So do we, if we ever catch for you.

consent

Agents propose, you decide

Your own consequential actions need you, outside the agent. Always.

undo

History, not just sync

Every version for 7 days, then daily for 30. Deletions don't spread.
