How it works

Set it up once, in a conversation. Then forget about it.

01

Point your agent

Your AI agent installs Casper and asks a couple of questions. You never touch a setting.

02

Choose your people

A friend sends you an invite, or you ask one. They give space; you keep the keys.

03

Confirm, and it's plumbing

You approve once, in Casper's own dialog. From then on every change is mirrored within seconds.

Trust

Your friends hold your files. They can never read them.

Encrypted before it leaves

Names, folders and contents are sealed on your Mac. Not your friends, not us — no one else can open them.

You decide, not your agent

Anything consequential waits for your yes, in a dialog your agent can't click.

Undo anything

Every version for a week, then daily for a month. Ask for yesterday's file and it comes back.

Where this goes

Mirroring is the first thing friends can share.

Casper is building the trust layer for people who look after each other's computers — and each other's agents. Give space, be known for it. Default to generosity.
